Brand and press.

Carbon’s identity is one yellow mark on graphite: a carbon ring that opens into a C. Download the files you need, and keep to the few rules below.

Logo files

Lockup

The mark and wordmark side by side. There is no vertical lockup: where the space is square, use the mark alone.

  • For dark backgroundsSVG, Carbon lockup: yellow mark and paper wordmark
  • Black, one colourSVG, Carbon lockup in black
  • All ink, for yellowSVG, Carbon lockup in ink

Mark

A hexagon, the six-atom carbon ring, with its right edge removed so it reads as a C.

  • Yellow, for dark backgroundsSVG, Carbon mark, yellow
  • Black, one colourSVG, Carbon mark, black
  • White, one colourSVG, Carbon mark, white

Wordmark

Custom-drawn and always lowercase. Use these files rather than typing it in a font.

  • Paper, for dark backgroundsSVG, Carbon wordmark, paper
  • Black, one colourSVG, Carbon wordmark, black
  • White, one colourSVG, Carbon wordmark, white

App icon

The yellow mark on a graphite plate. Never on a yellow plate.

  • 512 × 512 pxPNG, Carbon app icon

Colour

One yellow, used sparingly, on graphite and paper. The neutrals carry a trace of the yellow’s hue, so the two read as one family. There is no secondary brand colour.

  • Carbon Yellow#FAE91Doklch(92% 0.19 104)Primary
  • Yellow Deep#E4D530oklch(86% 0.17 104)Pressed or hover on yellow
  • Ink#10100Doklch(17% 0.006 107)Text on yellow, darkest surface
  • Paper#EFEFE6oklch(95% 0.012 107)Light text on dark, light surface
  • Graphite 950#090907oklch(14% 0.005 107)Page background
  • Graphite 900#181714oklch(20.5% 0.006 92)Surface
  • Graphite 800#22221Eoklch(25% 0.007 107)Raised surface
  • Graphite 700#33332Eoklch(32% 0.009 107)Lines, plates
  • Graphite 500#5E5E58oklch(48% 0.009 107)Secondary text on light
  • Graphite 300#9F9F96oklch(70% 0.013 107)Muted text on dark

Yellow works

  • As a fill: buttons, the mark, active states.
  • As text on Graphite 700 and darker.
  • With Ink as the only text colour on it.

Yellow never

  • Sits on white, Paper or busy photos.
  • Becomes text, an icon or a focus ring on a light background. Use Ink there.
  • Carries white or grey text.

Using the logo

Do

  • Use the supplied files.
  • Put the yellow mark on graphite or Ink, and the Ink version on yellow.
  • Use the black or white versions over photos and in one-colour print.
  • Scale proportionally, and keep the clear space and minimum sizes.

Don’t

  • Rotate, skew, stretch, outline, bevel or extrude the mark, or add a shadow or gradient.
  • Recolour it outside the palette, or put two colours inside it.
  • Put the mark in a yellow square or circle.
  • Retype the wordmark in a font, or track it out.
  • Rearrange the lockup, or build new shapes or letters from the mark.

Clear space

Keep a margin of X clear on every side of the mark and the lockup. X is a quarter of the mark’s height H, the same as the height of the C’s opening. No text, edges or other logos go inside it.

Describing Carbon

Carbon is a free, open-source desktop music app for Windows, macOS and Linux: Spotify, YouTube Music, your Navidrome, Jellyfin or Plex server, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, radio and local files in one window, with word-synced lyrics.

Spotify and YouTube marks belong to their owners. Where you show them, follow their own guidelines, and never blend them into Carbon’s mark.

Need something else? Email support@getcarbon.cc.
