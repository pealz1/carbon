Privacy, in
plain words.

Last updated 8 November 2026

Carbon has no servers and collects no analytics. It talks directly from your PC to the services below, and only for the reasons given.

Spotify sign-in

You sign in with Spotify, on Spotify’s own page. Carbon never sees your password. The sign-in tokens Carbon receives are stored locally on your PC in Windows Credential Manager.

Your music, library and playback come from Spotify and are covered by Spotify’s privacy policy.

Lyrics (LRCLIB)

To find synced lyrics, Carbon sends the current track’s title, artist, album and duration to LRCLIB.

Discord

If the Discord desktop app is running, Carbon passes what’s playing to it on your PC so your profile can show “Listening to…”. Discord then shares that status the way it normally does.

Last.fm

Last.fm receives scrobbles (the tracks you play) only if you connect your Last.fm account in Carbon’s Settings.

Updates (GitHub)

To check for new versions, Carbon contacts GitHub, where Carbon’s releases are published.

This website

getcarbon.cc uses no analytics and sets no cookies of its own. It is hosted on Cloudflare and loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so those services receive standard request information such as your IP address.
