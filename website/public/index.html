Your music,
in liquid glass.

Carbon is a free, open-source Spotify client for Windows 10 and 11. Built in Rust, it plays your library itself, fills lyrics word by word and keeps your sign-in on your PC.

Why Carbon

Press and hold any panel for the details.

Plays music itself

Spotify’s official Web Playback SDK runs inside Carbon, so no Spotify app has to stay open.

GlasshouseMara Ilse 1:123:48

Play, pause, seek, shuffle, repeat, your queue and volume sit in one glass bar, and playback carries on from the tray when you close the window. Requires Spotify Premium.

Liquid lyrics

Time-synced lyrics fill word by word as they’re sung.

Click any line to jump to that moment in the song. Lyrics come from LRCLIB, an open, community-maintained lyrics library.

Private by design

No ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your Spotify sign-in stays in Windows Credential Manager.

You sign in on Spotify’s own page, so Carbon never sees your Spotify password. A Carbon account stores only your email and a salted password hash.

      Questions, answered.

      Something else? Ask on GitHub or email support@getcarbon.cc.

      Do I need Spotify Premium?

      Yes. Carbon plays music through Spotify’s official Web Playback SDK, which works only with Premium accounts. Spotify also requires Premium to create the developer app used during setup.

      Do I need a Carbon account?

      Yes. Once Carbon accounts launch, a free Carbon account will be required to use the app. It’s separate from Spotify: an email address and a password, with a code emailed to you each time you sign in.

      Why do I need my own Spotify Client ID?

      Spotify’s 2026 developer rules limit each developer app to five users, so a shared ID would fill up almost immediately. You create your own instead. It’s free and takes about two minutes with the setup guide.

      Is my Spotify login safe?

      You sign in on Spotify’s own page, so your Spotify password never reaches Carbon. The token Carbon receives stays on your PC in Windows Credential Manager, and Carbon never needs your Client secret. The privacy policy has the details.

      How can I support Carbon?

      Carbon is free, always, and open source under the MIT license. Donations, once or monthly, open soon and will go toward keeping it maintained. They won’t unlock anything in the app, because everything is already free. Until then, ideas and feedback in GitHub Discussions help the most.

      Carbon is free, always, and open source under the MIT license. You can donate once or monthly to keep it maintained. A donation doesn’t unlock anything in the app, because everything is already free. Ideas and feedback in GitHub Discussions help too.

      Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

      The installer isn’t signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet, so Windows may warn you the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway. App updates are signed separately and verified before they install.

      Is there a Mac, Linux or phone version?

      Not yet. Carbon is built for Windows 10 and 11 today, and Carbon for mobile is in development. Follow progress on GitHub.
