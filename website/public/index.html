        Questions.

        Something else? Ask on GitHub or email support@getcarbon.cc.

        Do I need Spotify Premium?

        For Spotify, yes. Carbon plays Spotify through its official Web Playback SDK, which only works with Premium, and Spotify requires Premium to create the developer app used in setup. YouTube Music, your own server, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, radio and local files don’t need Spotify at all.

        Do I need a Carbon account?

        Yes, a free one: an email address and a password, separate from Spotify. The first time you sign in on a device, we email you a code. Create an account.

        Why do I need my own Spotify Client ID?

        Spotify limits each developer app to five users, so a shared ID would fill up at once. You make your own instead. It’s free and takes about two minutes with the setup guide.

        Is my Spotify login safe?

        You sign in on Spotify’s own page, so your Spotify password never reaches Carbon. The token Carbon gets stays in Windows Credential Manager or the macOS Keychain, and Carbon never needs your Client secret. Details are in the privacy policy.

        I use Spicetify, Cider or a YouTube Music app. What carries over?

        Your Spotify and YouTube Music libraries live in your accounts, so they show up as soon as you sign in. The switching guide covers what each app’s features map to in Carbon.

        Why does Windows show a SmartScreen warning?

        The installer isn’t signed with a paid code-signing certificate yet, so Windows may warn you the first time. Choose More info, then Run anyway. App updates are signed separately and verified before they install.

        Which Macs can run Carbon?

        Any Mac with macOS 11 or later and Safari 16.4 or newer, Apple silicon or Intel. The universal disk image covers both. The app is ad-hoc signed, so Gatekeeper may show a warning.

        How can I support Carbon?

        Donate once or monthly, report bugs, or share ideas in GitHub Discussions. Donations don’t unlock anything: everything is already free.
