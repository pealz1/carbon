# Music providers and playlist transfers

Open **More music** in Carbon to search a catalog or paste a public song, album, set or playlist link. **Open player** opens the provider’s own player. Its account and regional playback rules apply. **Import playlist** reads the available track list and lets you review matches before creating a Carbon playlist. Unmatched songs are displayed for manual search. Music files stay with their original service.

| Provider | What you can use |
| --- | --- |
| Apple Music | Search songs and albums and open Apple’s official player. Add a [MusicKit developer token](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/applemusicapi/generating-developer-tokens) in Apple Music provider settings to search catalog playlists, view their tracks and import them directly. For a personal playlist you can also export XML or text in Apple Music. [Apple’s export instructions](https://support.apple.com/guide/music/mus27cd5060f/mac) |
| SoundCloud | Paste a public track or set link to use its official widget, or open SoundCloud to search and sign in. No Carbon API key is needed. Embedded playback and imports require the uploader to allow the widget. [SoundCloud widget](https://developers.soundcloud.com/docs/api/html5-widget) |
| SoundCloud account API | Sign in through a registered SoundCloud app to search, browse playlists, likes and recent plays, and create or edit owned playlists. Register the redirect `http://127.0.0.1:8888/soundcloud-callback`, enter the app's client ID and secret in **SoundCloud → Provider settings**, then choose **Sign in to SoundCloud**. Carbon stores credentials in the operating system credential store. [Register an app](https://developers.soundcloud.com/docs/api/register-app.html) |
| TIDAL | Use **Search → TIDAL** to browse tracks, albums, artists and playlists, read your library, or create and edit owned playlists after signing in. Register a TIDAL app with `http://127.0.0.1:8888/tidal-callback` and enter its client ID. Playback uses TIDAL's official embeds and the listener's subscription. [TIDAL authorization](https://developer.tidal.com/documentation/api-sdk/api-sdk-authorization) · [Playback guidelines](https://developer.tidal.com/documentation/guidelines/guidelines-design-guidelines) |
| Deezer | Search public songs, albums and playlists. Open them in Deezer’s player and view or import collection tracks in Carbon. [Deezer](https://www.deezer.com/) |
| Mixcloud | Search public shows, open the player, and view or import public playlist contents. [Mixcloud](https://www.mixcloud.com/developers/) |
| Internet Archive | Search public recordings, view an item’s audio list and open its player. **Show downloadable audio** lists files from items with a public-domain or Creative Commons license. Saved recordings appear under **Offline music** and **Local files**. [Internet Archive audio](https://archive.org/details/audio) |
| Spotify | Connect Spotify for eligible library and playback features. Use the official player for public playlists. If Spotify withholds the rows, import an exported CSV, tab-separated track list or extended M3U file. [Spotify’s playlist changes](https://developer.spotify.com/documentation/web-api/tutorials/february-2026-migration-guide) |
| YouTube Music | Open a track, album or playlist and use Carbon’s YouTube player. Start radio from a playlist’s first available track. Sign in to edit your playlists and see the updated songs in Carbon. |

Bandcamp, radio, local files and personal Plex, Jellyfin and Navidrome servers are also available. Carbon keeps downloaded recordings on the device. **Profile and sync** lets you upload a Carbon playlist containing Spotify or YouTube songs after signing in. Sharing a playlist or a profile is optional. Local file paths, audio files, and service credentials are excluded from sharing.

Turn on automatic playlist sync in **Profile and sync** to pick up changes from another device. If both copies changed, open the online revision and review it. Listening history sync is optional and includes up to 5,000 plays from Spotify and YouTube. Private sessions pause syncing.
