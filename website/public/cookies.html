Cookies and browser storage

Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version: getcarbon.cc uses two cookies, both essential for signing in, and none for analytics or advertising. It keeps a few small items in your browser’s storage. Only one of them is optional, a record of how you first found Carbon, and it is kept only if you choose Allow. The Carbon app uses no cookies and no analytics. This notice adds detail to the Privacy Policy.

About this notice

Cookies are small files a website asks your browser to keep. Browser storage (local storage and session storage) works in a similar way, but only the website’s own scripts can read it. This notice lists every cookie and storage item that getcarbon.cc and Carbon’s account service at api.getcarbon.cc use, why, and for how long.

Cookies

We use two cookies, and both are essential: without them you could not stay signed in. Both are set by api.getcarbon.cc, are HttpOnly and Secure (page scripts can’t read them, and they only travel over HTTPS), and neither is used for analytics or advertising.

CookieWhat it doesHow long it lasts
__Host-carbon_sessionKeeps you signed in to your Carbon account.Up to 30 days after your last visit, and never more than 90 days after you signed in. Signing out ends it.
__Host-carbon_deviceRemembers a browser where you finished a sign-in with an emailed code, so it can skip the code next time.90 days.

Because these cookies are strictly necessary for a service you ask for, we do not ask for consent to use them.

Browser storage

The website keeps these items in your browser. They stay on your device; the website’s scripts read them, but they are not sent to us as cookies are.

ItemWhat it doesHow long it lasts
carbon.signedIn
local storage		Notes that you have signed in before, so the site knows to check whether you still are.Until you sign out or the site finds you are signed out.
carbon.consent
local storage		Your answer to the visit prompt: Allow or Reject.Until you change it or clear the site’s data.
carbon.firstVisit
local storage, only if you choose Allow		Where you first came from: the linking address and campaign tags.Until you choose Reject or clear the site’s data.
carbon.email, carbon.sent, carbon.flash
session storage		Your email address, when a code was last sent, and a one-time status message, while you move between sign-up and sign-in pages.Until you close the tab.
carbon.seen

carbon.gh.latest, carbon.gh.repo, carbon.gh.releases

The visit prompt

The website counts visits as daily totals, without cookies, whatever you choose. Website visits explains exactly what is counted.

On your first visit, a prompt asks whether the site may remember how you found Carbon. It does not appear on sign-in, account or shared playlist pages.

  • Allow: your browser keeps carbon.firstVisit, so later visits count as returning, and a later download is credited to the link that first brought you here. Only the linking site’s name and campaign tags reach us.
  • Reject: nothing extra is stored, and any first-visit record is deleted.

Closing the prompt without choosing stores nothing extra. For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, the first-visit record relies on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Privacy signals

If your browser sends Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, the website treats it as a Reject. It sends no visit information at all, stores no first-visit record and does not show the prompt. The essential sign-in cookies still work, because you ask for them when you sign in.

Other services the site contacts

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies, and we do not embed third-party trackers. To load its pages, the website contacts:

  • Cloudflare, which serves the website and the account service;
  • Google Fonts, which serves the website’s fonts;
  • GitHub’s public API, when the download menu looks up the latest release;
  • Spotify’s and YouTube’s image servers, when a shared playlist page shows cover art.

Each of them receives standard request information, such as your IP address and browser type, and handles it under its own privacy policy. Donation pages are run by Stripe on its own site, under Stripe’s own cookie policy.

The Carbon app

The Carbon desktop app has no analytics, no tracking and no advertising identifiers. It keeps your sign-in tokens in your system’s credential store and your settings on your device, as What stays on your device describes. When you sign in to the app, it opens this website in your browser, where the cookies above apply.

Changing your choice

  • Open to change your answer to the visit prompt at any time. The same button is at the bottom of every page.
  • Clear this site’s data in your browser’s settings to remove every item above. You will be signed out.
  • Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser to refuse optional storage on every visit.
  • You can block cookies in your browser, but then you will not be able to sign in.

Changes to this notice

If we add, change or remove a cookie or storage item, we will update this notice and the date at the top. We will ask for your consent again before using anything optional that is new.
