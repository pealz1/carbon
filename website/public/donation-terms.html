Last updated November 24, 2026

The short version: a donation is a voluntary gift. It does not grant access. Carbon is not a registered charity, so donations are not tax-deductible. Stripe, or GitHub Sponsors when offered, handles the payment. You can cancel a monthly donation at any time. We refund mistakes reported within 14 days, charges you didn’t authorize, and wherever the law requires it. These terms are part of the Terms of Service.

About these terms

These terms apply when you donate to Carbon, once or monthly, through the links on getcarbon.cc or through GitHub Sponsors when it is offered. Carbon is an independent project by pealz; “Carbon”, “we” and “us” mean that project and the person who runs it. By donating, you agree to these terms and to the Terms of Service, which include them. Where the two differ about donations, these terms control.

A gift, not a purchase

Every feature in Carbon is free for everyone, and it stays that way. A donation is a voluntary gift that helps keep the project going:

  • You receive nothing in exchange: no goods, no services, no features, no priority support, no influence over the project and no other benefit.
  • Donating does not change how Carbon or your account works, and not donating never limits it.
  • A donation is not an investment, a loan, a pre-order or a payment for future work. It does not create any partnership, ownership or other relationship between you and Carbon.
  • A thank-you, such as a listing on the Supporters wall, is a courtesy, not something you paid for.

Who can donate

  • You must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live. Younger supporters may donate only with a parent or guardian’s permission and payment method.
  • You must use a payment method that you own or are authorized to use.
  • You may not donate if you are in, or ordinarily live in, a country or region under comprehensive US sanctions, or if you are named on a US or other applicable list of sanctioned or restricted parties.
  • You may not donate money that comes from crime, or donate to test stolen cards or move money for someone else.

We may refuse, cancel or return any donation, for example if it looks fraudulent, if accepting it would break the law, or if it comes with conditions we cannot accept.

How payment works

  • Processors. Donations on getcarbon.cc are processed by Stripe on Stripe’s own pages. Donations through GitHub Sponsors, when offered, are processed by GitHub. Their terms and privacy policies apply to the payment. We never receive or store your card details.
  • Amount and currency. You choose the amount, subject to any minimum the processor sets. You are charged in the currency shown at checkout. If that differs from your card’s currency, your bank may convert it and charge a fee.
  • Fees. We do not add any fee of our own. The processor deducts its fees from what we receive, so we get a little less than you give.
  • Confirmation. A donation is complete when the processor confirms the payment. If a payment fails, you are not charged; for a monthly donation, the processor may try again.

Monthly donations

When you choose Monthly, you authorize the processor to charge the same amount to your payment method once a month, starting on the day you sign up, until you cancel.

How to cancel

  • Choose “Manage a monthly donation” in the Support section of the home page. It opens Stripe’s customer portal, where you can cancel or update your card.
  • Use the link in your Stripe receipt email.
  • For GitHub Sponsors, cancel from your sponsorships on GitHub.
  • Or email support@getcarbon.cc from the address you donated with, and we will cancel it for you.

Cancelling stops future payments. Payments already made are not refunded, except as described in Refunds. If Carbon stops accepting donations, we will cancel every monthly donation so no further payments are taken.

Receipts and taxes

  • Receipts. Stripe emails you a receipt for each payment, and GitHub shows yours in your sponsorships. A receipt is a record of your payment, not a tax receipt.
  • Not tax-deductible. Carbon is not a registered charity or tax-exempt organization anywhere, so donations are not tax-deductible, and we cannot issue charitable tax receipts.
  • Your taxes. You are responsible for any taxes that apply to you as the giver. Please do not treat a donation as a business expense for services received, because none are provided.

Refunds

Donations are generally non-refundable, because they are gifts. We refund a donation when:

  • You made a mistake, such as the wrong amount, a duplicate payment, or Monthly when you meant Once, and you email support@getcarbon.cc within 14 days of the payment;
  • You did not authorize the charge, for example if your card was used without your permission. Tell us and your bank as soon as you notice;
  • The law requires it.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, through the processor. Your bank may take a few days to show them. We keep a record of each refund, as the Privacy Policy describes.

Chargebacks

Please contact us before asking your bank to reverse a donation. We can usually fix the problem faster, and every chargeback costs the project a fee.

If you file a chargeback for a donation you made and authorized, we may contest it by giving the processor the payment record. We may also refuse future donations from you and remove you from the Supporters wall. Filing false chargebacks can be fraud, and we may report it.

The Supporters wall

The Supporters wall on getcarbon.cc thanks people who donate and ask to be listed. It is opt-in: we never list you unless you ask.

  • To be listed, email support@getcarbon.cc after donating, with the name and the optional link you want shown. The wall does not show amounts.
  • We may decline or shorten a name or link that breaks the Acceptable Use Policy, impersonates someone or points somewhere unsafe.
  • Ask at any time and we will remove you. The website’s files are public on GitHub, so an earlier listing may remain in the repository’s history after we remove it from the site.

How donations are used

Donations go to the project and the person who maintains it, and pay for the hours that keep Carbon working. We decide how to use them. We do not promise any particular feature, release, level of support or timeline in return, and if Carbon is paused or ends, donations already made are not refunded.

Your information

From the processor we receive your name, your email address (GitHub shares it only if you allow it), the amount and the date of each donation. We use it to keep records, handle refunds and cancellations, and thank you. We keep donation records as long as accounting law requires, typically up to 7 years. The Privacy Policy has the details.

Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. We will post the new version here and update the date at the top. A change applies to donations made after it is posted. If a change materially affects a monthly donation, we will tell you by email before your next payment, so you can cancel first.

Contact

Questions about a donation, a refund or the Supporters wall? Email support@getcarbon.cc.
