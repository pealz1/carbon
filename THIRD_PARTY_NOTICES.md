## ytmusicapi

Carbon's YouTube Music support ports request shapes and response-parsing logic from
[sigma67/ytmusicapi](https://github.com/sigma67/ytmusicapi) (search filter parameters, the
`WEB_REMIX` client context, the `SAPISIDHASH` authorization header, playlist edit actions and the
layout of browse, search and watch-next responses). The ported code lives in
`src-tauri/src/youtube/` and `src/lib/youtube/`.
